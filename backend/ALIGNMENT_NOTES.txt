Blue Fit Backend - aligned/no-AI revision

- Prior alignment/build/QA decisions in the conversation

2. Existing template CRUD/assignment/scheduling routes are preserved.

5. AI image upload/generate/approve routes were removed because the AI image feature was explicitly excluded.

No unrelated backend architecture was changed.
